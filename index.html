ABOUT

Varutzu is a Romanian producer and Visual Artist, He is well known as TutisiSpargatorDeCur, his main Musical Project for fun beats and funny songs.

Varutzu is a small artist and producer, he started the music production in the may of 2024 with the EP "Urmaritorul De Emoloaice" then he did better releases like "Homo", "Burn it to pieces", etc. He is still active as a Producer and Visual Artist.
